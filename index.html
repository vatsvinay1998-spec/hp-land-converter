Kanal standard (karam)
57.5-inch karam in use. Kanal, marla and sarsahi are about 24% smaller than the standard. Older Punjab settlements (Una, reported for Kangra) used this. Confirm with the Patwari or use the hectare figure on the Jamabandi.

Rakba figures on the Jamabandi are positional, not decimal. Enter each part separately.

System A

Bigha · Biswa · Biswansi

बीघा · बिस्वा · बिस्वांसी

Sirmaur (Paonta Sahib), Shimla, Solan, Mandi, Bilaspur, Chamba, Kullu, Kinnaur, Lahaul & Spiti

  • 20 biswansi = 1 biswa
  • 20 biswa = 1 bigha
  • 1 bigha = 809.371 m² = 8,712 ft²
  • 5 bigha = 1 acre
System B

Kanal · Marla · Sarsahi

कनाल · मरला · सरसाही

Kangra, Una, Hamirpur, and pockets such as Nalagarh — go by the unit printed on the Jamabandi

  • 9 sarsahi = 1 marla
  • 20 marla = 1 kanal
  • 1 kanal = 505.857 m² = 5,445 ft²
  • 8 kanal = 1 acre (1 ghumaon)

Crossing the two systems

  • 1 bigha = 1.6 kanal = 32 marla
  • 1 kanal = 0.625 bigha = 12.5 biswa
  • 1 acre = 5 bigha = 8 kanal
  • 1 hectare = 12.3553 bigha = 19.7684 kanal

Based on the standard 66-inch karam.
